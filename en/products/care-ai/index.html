Capabilities and outputs

Agent labels below organize the source capabilities for this website; they do not imply separate commercial product launches.

Research outcome

VOC Structuring Agent

Structures complaints into facts, original goals, appraisal and current requests.

Input
VOC text and business taxonomy
Output
Four core fields and five classification axes: business, product, stage, issue and situation

Research outcome

Emotion & Response Analysis Agent

Compares expressed emotions with response characteristics to identify service improvements.

Input
VOC, responses and satisfaction feedback
Output
Comparisons of direct answers, request outcomes and alternative guidance by emotion

Research outcome

Recurring VOC & FAQ Analysis Agent

Groups recurring requests and compares them with existing FAQs to find coverage gaps.

Input
VOC records and existing FAQs
Output
Recurring request clusters, FAQ coverage and improvement candidates

Review function implemented

Message Quality Review Agent

Checks outbound messages against a rubric and proposes revisions for staff review.

Input
Draft messages and a quality rubric
Output
Checks for purpose, body, submission channel, considerate wording, closing and greeting; gaps and revision suggestions

Follow-up research, not completed capabilities

Record writing, history linking, procedure guidance, follow-up management and quality review in a multi-agent counseling workflow remain proposed follow-up research. Dynamic FAQ implementation, combined loan-limit calculations and progress tracking are improvement candidates. Satisfaction, complaint reduction and time savings require further validation.
