How the teams work together

When defects increase

Defect reviews patterns and candidate causes. Quality coordinates corrective actions, Maintenance reviews servicing needs, and Action prepares a proposal for approval. Audit organizes the evidence.

When materials arrive late

Material identifies supply risks, Planning reviews the sequence, and Simulation compares alternatives. Approval records the planner’s decision; Logistics and Fleet review transport changes.

Illustrative workflows based on role definitions; end-to-end availability depends on integration and validation.

Start with a defined operational scope

Current demonstrations primarily use simulated data with limited live pipeline integration. Site-specific data connections, approval policies and equipment execution require scoping and validation. These roles describe the solution design, not 28 independently deployed services.
