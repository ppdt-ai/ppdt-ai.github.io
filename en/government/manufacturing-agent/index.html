Manufacturing AI Agent Pilot & Scale-up Programs

From watching data to a shop floor that decides and acts on its own

Deploy manufacturing AI agents that run shop-floor data through a Perceive–Plan–Act–Learn loop, aligned with government pilot and scale-up programs. Purple Delta's A³ Framework–based Manufacturing Intelligence Agent connects to MES, ERP and equipment systems to automate anomaly detection, root-cause analysis and action recommendations.

Who this is for

  • Manufacturers joining AI adoption or pilot programs (manufacturing AI, industrial AI agents)
  • Plants that already get anomaly alerts but still rely on people for analysis and action
  • Companies that want an automated operating-decision layer across MES, ERP and equipment data
  • Institutions and consortia that need a solution partner for manufacturing AI projects

What Purple Delta provides

Pilot design

Break shop-floor problems into agent-solvable tasks and define pilot goals and evaluation metrics.

Manufacturing Intelligence Agent build

Configure an agent for real-time quality prediction, anomaly detection, root-cause analysis and action recommendations on your data.

System integration (tool calling)

Expose MES, ERP and equipment APIs as agent tools for automated lookups, records and notifications.

Approval queue & guardrails

Route critical actions through human approval with guardrails designed for safe operation.

EvalOps-based evaluation

Continuously measure and improve decision accuracy, response time and action impact.

Scale-up package

Templates and operating guides that let pilot results be reapplied to other lines and plants.
